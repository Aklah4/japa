Am I eligible?

Common Eligibility Requirements

While every application is unique, these are the core criteria most visa streams require. Book a free assessment for a definitive answer.

🇨🇦 Canada — Express Entry
Federal Skilled Worker Program
  • Minimum 1 year of skilled work experience (NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3)
  • Language proficiency: CLB 7+ in all four abilities
  • Educational credential assessment (ECA) from a designated body
  • Proof of settlement funds (unless offered a Canadian job)
  • Admissibility — no serious criminal or medical inadmissibility
  • Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score competitive for current draws
🇬🇧 United Kingdom — Skilled Worker
Points-Based Immigration System
  • Job offer from a UK Home Office licensed sponsor employer
  • Role must be at RQF Level 3 or above (A-level equivalent)
  • Salary of at least £26,200 or the going rate for the role
  • English language proficiency (B1 level CEFR)
  • Sufficient funds to support yourself (£1,270 in savings)
  • 70 points total achievable across all mandatory and tradeable criteria
🇦🇺 Australia — Subclass 189
Skilled Independent Visa
  • Occupation on the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL)
  • Positive skills assessment from the relevant assessing authority
  • Age under 45 years at time of invitation
  • English proficiency: Competent English (IELTS 6.0 in each band)
  • Points score of at least 65 in SkillSelect
  • Meet health and character requirements
🇦🇺 Australia — Subclass 190
Skilled Nominated Visa
  • Nomination by an Australian state or territory government
  • Occupation on the relevant state's skilled occupation list
  • Commitment to live and work in the nominating state for 2 years
  • Points score of at least 65 (nomination adds 5 points)
  • English proficiency and positive skills assessment required
  • Genuine intention to settle in the nominating state
